01Key findings

Perimeter and executive summary from the original report.

01

Perimeter

The perimeter studied by RECMA includes the six international media groups. The total market studied is estimated by RECMA to be $2,417m in Overall Activity Volume.

02

Market trend

After a soar in 2023, RECMA estimates an increase of the Hong Kong market in 2024 (+10%). Over three years, the increase is 43%.

03

Non-traditional activity

Traditional media is about $805m and the average share of non-traditional activity is increasing by +3pts: 67% in 2024 from 64% in 2023.

04

Top 5 agencies in 2024

For 2024, OMD leads ahead of Starcom. PHD and Zenith rank #3, followed by Carat. The Top 5 agencies represent 59% of the total market studied.

05

Group ranking in 2024

Excluding independent agencies, OMG is leading with 25.8% industry share ahead of Publicis Media at 24.5%.

02Methodology

Overall Activity is RECMA’s unique metric for ranking media agencies quantitatively.

RECMA asked agencies to declare staff number, Overall Activity growth rate and non-traditional activity share. Growth rates are checked with account moves, monthly prorate and agency client lists. RECMA applies a 78% deflator on monitored figures by Admango. Overall Activity includes traditional media, online paid media and diversified services such as digital, performance, social/content, data & analytics, e-commerce and international coordination.

OMD

+20%

OMD saw strong performance in 2024, supported by new business wins like Maxim's and increased investment in digital (+6pts). While traditional media spend decreased for Beiersdorf and Octopus, this was offset by growth from clients such as HKTDC, Vita Green, and McDonald’s. The departure of Estée Lauder had limited impact.

Starcom incl. Spark

+31%

Starcom maintained strong momentum in 2024, thanks to major client wins such as FrieslandCampina and Pacific Century, sustained growth for P&G, and full-year impact of accounts gained in 2023.

PHD

+10%

PHD continued to grow in 2024, supported by new business wins including Trip.com, Vitasoy and Mox, plus accounts gained in 2023 such as Tanjai and WeChat Pay.

Zenith

+13%

Zenith sustained its positive momentum in 2024, building on the full-year impact of AIA and continued growth from clients such as Walt Disney and Deliveroo.

Carat

+9%

Carat maintained its growth trajectory in 2024, supported by new client additions such as Swatch and Swire, plus several wins from 2023.

dentsu X

+5%

dentsu X maintained steady growth in 2024, supported by Prada and Wai Yuen Tong, while building on 2023 momentum.

EssenceMediacom

+5%

EssenceMediacom sustained positive momentum with Hang Seng Bank and Zero Finance wins, plus increased investment from Uber and Mars.

Mindshare

-9%

Mindshare declined in 2024, impacted by the full-year effect of past losses and additional departures such as Foodpanda and Suntory.

Wavemaker

-7%

Wavemaker faced a more challenging 2024, impacted by key departures such as BBK Electronics, Swatch and Friesland.

Havas Media

+20%

Havas maintained positive momentum in 2024, supported by new wins such as Inchcape, Emma Sleep and Venetian Cotai, plus HK Jockey Club expansion.

UM incl. Initiative

-10%

Mediabrands posted a slight decline in 2024. New business wins did not fully offset upcoming departures like Amazon, Lego and Kenvue.

iProspect

+11%

iProspect sustained growth, building on recent client wins such as Pizza Hut, PHD and KFC.
