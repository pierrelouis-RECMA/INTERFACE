01Key Findings

Executive market read.

Publicis Media leads the market.

Spark Foundry is the top individual winner at +$42m NBB, driven by the Nestlé + Wyeth consolidation.

dentsu X posts a strong ratio.

A near-clean sweep of +$27m, mainly powered by Telford, with a significant retained book including KAO and Ocean Park.

Omnicom consolidates across agencies.

Hearts & Science, PHD, UM incl. Initiative and OMD show a combined positive position despite visible departures.

WPP Media faces pressure.

WPP Media shows a negative group NBB, while EssenceMediacom remains positive thanks to Webull.
